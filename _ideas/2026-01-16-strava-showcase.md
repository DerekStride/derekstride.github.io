# Strava Stats

I have a [Github project](https://github.com/derekstride/strava-stats) called [strava-stats](/strava-stats) which renders a static site showing all your workouts on a Calendar. It's easy to deploy and runs on a cron schedule to update peroidically.

Turning this into a subscription SasS project involves getting approved as a Strava Application. Then you could provide a similar experience to all strava athletes.
